This can be thought of as a chaotic attractor.
For example, iboga binding to receptors can induce a burst of GDNF, or even an autoregulatory feedback loop of this neurotrophic factor.
Also, aerobic exercise is known to stimulate GDNF. Entropy results in heat generation whereas when we exercise our muscles get cold (a form of theoretical "negative temperature impedance").

Note: This is a speculative observation. However, GDNF's role in neural survival and development is well-established. This framework hypothesizes that there are nonlinear dynamics that affect protein expression and folding, and potentially morphology and cell division/microtubule dynamic instability. Simulating differential equations that may theoretically explain these non-linear cascades and eigenstates can help us visualize potential protein dynamics. I am especially excited about applying the idea of these nonlinear protein dynamics to research in cell morphology and optogenetics. I would like to explore if these eigenstates or phononic phenomena (like chaotic attractor-like motion) in proteins may interact with ultra weak biophotons or stronger photons, potentially leading to exciton states.
